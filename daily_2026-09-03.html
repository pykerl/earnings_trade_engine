Why this trade?

10% of LULU's float is sold short and would take about 4.4 days of normal volume to buy back, and shorts were still adding at the last settlement. 4% of the float already trades hands daily, so forced short covering after a positive surprise has to chase a fast-moving stock. This is a lottery ticket, not an edge trade: the call can easily expire worthless and lose 100% of its cost — which is why it is capped at $250 and kept out of the main risk budget.
